Scoring

How your points are counted

  • Sign in and finish a test. The score saved is your percentage correct.
  • Today starts at midnight. The week starts Monday 00:00. The month starts on the 1st. Times are Lagos.
  • Only your first 3 tests each day count. A later test that day is saved, but it does not change your rank.
  • Points = average × 10 + improvement × 4 + days × 15.
  • Average is the mean of the tests that counted.
  • Improvement is the later half of those tests minus the earlier half. It starts once 4 tests have counted. A drop does not take points away.
  • Days is how many different days those counted tests fell on.
  • If points are tied, the higher average is ahead, then the student with more counted tests.
  • The chart counts every finished test. The rank does not count past 3 a day.
